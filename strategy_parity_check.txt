Source G/H rules preserved:
- FIRST V2 eligible: price 0.55..0.75, momentum 0.03..0.30, lookback 2 ticks.
- Consensus source: FIRST V2-eligible vote, NOT SAFE target pass.
- Target token never confirms itself.
- One other token counts at most once.
- Same-cycle votes share one decision timestamp.
- G: target 0.67..0.70, momentum +0.05..+0.10, >=2 DISTINCT OTHER
  same-side FIRST-V2 votes in previous 10 sec, one ENTRY, no DCA.
- H: exact same entry gate as G.
- H DCA: arm ask <=0.50; no buy on arm tick; later ask 0.30..0.60,
  rebound +0.05..+0.15, elapsed <=120 sec, one DCA only.
- No side switching.
- No strategy stop-loss.
